JustHeal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 App: JustHeal (Android package in.vitacasahealth.justheal) Operated by: VitaCasaHealth Services (“VitaCasaHealth”, “we”, “us”) — vitacasahealth.in

JustHeal connects people who need in-home nursing and caregiving with caregivers, and lets hospitals, rehab centres and clinics post caregiver requirements. This policy applies to the JustHeal Android app and explains what information it and its related admin tools collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the controls you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past — if anything here is unclear, write to us (section 13).

1. Summary

  • We collect only what is needed to register you, verify caregivers, post and match care requirements, and keep the service safe.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, SMS, or microphone audio.
  • We share data only with the people involved in a match (section 5) and the service providers that run the app (section 6). No other third party receives it.
  • Sensitive information (caregiver identity documents, a patient’s health details) is shared only with the people who need it to run a match, as described in section 5.
  • Your account data, requirements and documents are stored in India.
  • Your data is encrypted in transit and encrypted at rest (section 11).
  • You can view and update your details, and delete your account from inside the app (section 9).

2. Who this policy applies to

JustHeal has three kinds of accounts, all using the same app:

  • Caregivers — nurses and care workers who register to find work.
  • Patients / families (“individuals”) — people who post a requirement for care for themselves or a family member.
  • Organisations — hospitals, rehab centres and clinics that post caregiver requirements.

It also covers our staff-only admin website, which our team uses to verify caregivers, review postings, and support users.

  • Details about the person needing care, which you enter when you post a requirement: patient’s name, age, gender, weight, city and area, medical conditions, toilet-assistance and feeding needs, hours of care needed, preferred start date and duration, and your preferences for the caregiver (gender, language, religion).

Organisations (additionally)

  • Organisation name and type (hospital / rehab / clinic), contact person name, city and area, and the requirements you post (type of nurse, vacancies, accommodation/food provided, skills, duration).

Everyone

  • “Forgot PIN” requests (we record the phone number and the request so our staff can call and help you).
  • The reason you give when you decline or reject an applicant.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you job alerts, reminders and messages from our team.
  • Technical and security records: the time of your requests, your IP address and device/browser type (user agent) when you log in or take certain actions, and the outcome of failed login attempts (the phone number entered and the reason it failed — never the PIN or one-time code that was tried). Failed-login records are deleted after 30 days.
  • Error reports: when the service hits an unexpected error, we store the technical details of that request so we can fix it. Sensitive fields (PINs, passwords, tokens, one-time codes, Aadhaar-related and similar secrets) are removed before storage, and error reports are deleted after 7 days.
  • Basic usage activity: which screens and buttons are used (for example “opened Jobs”, “tapped Rate Card”) and when you were last active. We use this to find where people get stuck and to help users who ask for support. These events carry no free-text you typed, and are deleted after 90 days.
  • App version and platform, to tell you when an update is required.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and the app does not read your location.

5. Who can see your information

JustHeal only works if the right people can see the right details. Please read this section carefully.

  • Caregivers browsing jobs can see the care requirement details a patient/family or organisation posted — the care needs, location (city and area), schedule and salary guidance. They cannot see the poster’s phone number until the poster (or our team) accepts them.
  • Patients/families and organisations reviewing applicants can see the full caregiver profile of someone who applied to their requirement — including name, phone number, email, age, gender, qualification, languages, religion, and the documents the caregiver uploaded (selfie, Aadhaar document, qualification and other documents). By applying to a requirement, a caregiver agrees to this. Do not apply if you do not want a particular poster to see these.
  • Once a caregiver is accepted, the caregiver can see the contact name and phone number of the person who accepted them (the family, the organisation, or our staff member).
  • Our admin team (a small number of authorised VitaCasaHealth staff, each with their own login) can see and manage account information, documents, postings, applications and support requests, in order to verify caregivers, review postings for legitimacy, and give support. Admin actions are recorded in an internal audit log.
  • Other users never see your PIN, your device data, or your technical/security records.

We share information only as described in this policy, with the service providers in section 6, or when required by law (see section 7).

We use trusted providers to run JustHeal. They process data only on our instructions to provide their service.

Some services we use to deliver messages work on global networks — push notifications are delivered through Google’s infrastructure, and email and SMS through their providers’ systems — so the limited data needed to deliver a message (for example your notification token, or the phone number a one-time code is sent to) may pass through servers outside India. We choose providers that protect data to a standard comparable to this policy.

7. Legal reasons we may disclose information

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that it is required by law, court order or a lawful request from a public authority, or necessary to protect the safety of a person or to investigate fraud or misuse of the service.

8. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To verify caregivers (our team reviews the uploaded documents and may call you) before they can take work.
  • To let patients, families and organisations post requirements and review applicants, and to let caregivers browse and apply.
  • To send job alerts, reminders and admin messages you have enabled.
  • To give support, investigate problems and fix errors.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access, and to keep an audit trail of important actions.
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve the app.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not make decisions about you through automated profiling, and we do not use your data to train advertising or third-party AI models.

9. Your choices and controls

View and update your information. In the app’s Profile screen you can see your details and update what is editable — for example your phone number, login PIN and, for caregivers, qualification, languages and documents. Patients/families can edit their posted requirement at any time and cancel or reactivate it; organisations can edit or cancel theirs. Some caregiver fields (name, gender, religion) are locked after registration to protect the verification process; ask our team and we will change them for you. (A caregiver who changes their phone number or re-uploads their Aadhaar document is sent back for re-verification.)

Delete your account. Go to Profile → Delete My Account, then enter your 4-digit PIN to confirm. This is permanent. When you delete your account:

  • You are logged out and can no longer log in.
  • Your phone number, name, email, PIN and notification token are removed or replaced with anonymous placeholders.
  • For caregivers: your selfie, Aadhaar document, qualification document and other uploaded documents are removed from our records and deleted from file storage.
  • For patients/families and organisations: the care requirement(s) you posted are permanently deleted — including the patient’s name and medical and care details you entered — together with the applications caregivers made to them. If a caregiver had been accepted, they are released and can take other work.
  • For organisations: your contact person’s name is anonymised. The organisation’s name, type and city/area, which describe the business rather than a person, are kept as a business record.
  • You will stop receiving notifications.

What may remain after deletion. To keep the service auditable and secure, we keep an internal audit log of administrative and account actions, including a minimal entry that your account was deleted and when. Your phone number and name are removed from your account record, but older log entries about changes made to your account may still contain limited details such as a previous name. We also keep anything we are required to keep by law, only for as long as the law requires. If you believe something about you remains that should not, write to us (section 13); we will act on a verified request within 30 days and tell you if we cannot, and why.

Request deletion without the app. You do not need the app installed. Email vitacasahealthindia@gmail.com from any address with the subject “Delete my JustHeal account” and the phone number registered on the account. We will confirm it is you, usually by calling that number, and then delete the account and the data described above within 30 days. You can use this route if you have lost your PIN or your phone, or if you have uninstalled the app. You may also ask us to delete only part of your data (for example, a single uploaded document) without closing the account.

Access a copy of your data. You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you (section 13). We will verify it is you first, usually by calling the phone number on the account.

Correct your data. If something is wrong and you cannot change it in the app, tell us and we will correct it.

Notifications. Turn off notifications in your device’s app settings at any time; this does not affect your account.

Camera and file access. You can withdraw these in your device settings. The app will then ask again the next time you try to take a selfie or attach a document.

If you cannot use the app. You can make any of the above requests through the contact details in section 13, without installing the app.

11. Security

We protect information with measures including: encryption in transit (HTTPS) between the app and our servers; encryption at rest — our database and file-storage provider encrypts stored data, including uploaded documents, on disk; login PINs and admin passwords stored only as salted one-way hashes; uploaded documents kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived signed links; role-based access so staff see only what their role needs; every admin sign-in tied to an individual account; and an audit log of administrative actions. No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law. Choose a PIN that is not easy to guess and do not share it. If you suspect someone else has used your account, contact us immediately and we will help you reset your PIN.

12. Children

JustHeal is intended for adults. Caregivers must be at least 18 to register. A patient or family member who is a child may be the subject of a care requirement, but the account must be created and managed by a parent or guardian, who is responsible for the information entered. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
